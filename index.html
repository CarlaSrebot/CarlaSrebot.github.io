Carla Srebot

PhD Candidate, Vancouver School of Economics, University of British Columbia

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Vancouver School of Economics, University of British Columbia, and a PhD Fellow at the Stone Centre on Wealth and Income Inequality.

I am on the 2026–2027 job market.

My research focuses on labor markets in developing countries, with an emphasis on informality, gender, and job amenities. In my job market paper, I study why informality persists by separating the availability of formal employment from workers’ decisions to formalize. Combining administrative data from Brazilian labor inspections with a survey experiment, I show that most workers formalize when given the opportunity. I then examine how wages and non-wage job amenities shape take-up, and how these responses vary across workers and workplaces.
